# Archetype Presets

Each image uses **one Primary Archetype**. Secondary influence is allowed, but it may not replace the primary variable.

---

## 1. `path_space`

**Narrative thesis:** I decide where I can move and what path is mine.

**Primary Variable:** Space / Direction

**Coupled Pair:** Space × Shape

**Stable channels:** silhouette, directional gesture, value separation, movement path.

**Camera default:** follows first, then predicts as Agency increases.

**Color role:** identity accent and motion punctuation; color supports space rather than becoming the main emotional field.

**Edge role:** clear action edges; trailing or peripheral edges may soften to strengthen movement.

**Medium behavior:** graphic comic rendering, perspective-driven layering, directional line energy, selective halftone and print texture.

### State behavior
- **baseline:** navigable space, moderate depth, stable framing.
- **pressure:** usable space compresses; competing directions rise; framing tightens.
- **crisis:** high movement but few meaningful paths; perspective conflict peaks.
- **decision:** one clear direction appears; gesture simplifies around intent.
- **agency:** space opens; camera gives room in advance; shape and path align.
- **resolution:** stable open space with a residual sense of self-directed trajectory.

### Anti-rules
- Do not turn every state into extreme speed lines.
- Do not let background motion erase face readability.
- Do not use color as the primary engine when space should carry the story.

## 2. `boundary_emotion`

**Narrative thesis:** I decide what may cross the boundary between myself, others, and the world.

**Primary Variable:** Boundary / Edge

**Coupled Pair:** Edge × Color

**Stable channels:** gesture continuity, figure-ground relation, value anchor, selected edge behavior.

**Camera default:** gives the image room to breathe; closer framing is used for intimacy, not generic intensity.

**Color role:** emotional and relational field. Color may leave objects, occupy negative space, or bleed across boundaries.

**Edge role:** full Hard / Medium / Soft / Lost spectrum; edge changes must follow emotional or relational conditions.

**Medium behavior:** painterly watercolor fields, soft paper texture, controlled wash, dry-brush accents, graphic figure structure underneath.

### State behavior
- **baseline:** soft controlled boundary; balanced negative space.
- **pressure:** color fields separate; selected edges harden; figure-ground fragility rises.
- **crisis:** bleed and edge contradiction peak; background may become psychological field.
- **decision:** one relational boundary becomes legible and deliberate.
- **agency:** permeability is chosen rather than suffered; strong feeling with regained organization.
- **resolution:** softer stable field with emotional residue, not a reset to neutrality.

### Anti-rules
- Do not equate emotion with pink/purple palette.
- Do not blur the entire face.
- Do not let watercolor softness remove structural readability.

## 3. `rebellion_time`

**Narrative thesis:** I decide when I synchronize with the system and when I refuse it.

**Primary Variable:** Time / Medium

**Coupled Pair:** Time × Medium

**Stable channels:** high-contrast structural silhouette, global gesture, recurring identity mark, value skeleton.

**Camera default:** relatively stable reference frame so temporal / medium desynchronization remains legible.

**Color role:** registration relationship rather than emotional wash; color plates may shift independently from contour or texture.

**Edge role:** printed, torn, duplicated, rough or discontinuous locally while global body design stays coherent.

**Medium behavior:** collage, xerographic print, risograph offset, torn paper, screenprint, misregistration, zine energy, layered ink systems.

### State behavior
- **baseline:** controlled registration offset and medium variation.
- **pressure:** more layers update at different cadences; local mismatch increases.
- **crisis:** forced uniformity or uncontrolled fragmentation threatens identity; black/value skeleton must survive.
- **decision:** inconsistency becomes deliberate rather than reactive.
- **agency:** synchronization/desynchronization becomes a chosen graphic weapon.
- **resolution:** personal rhythm remains, but it is organized enough to read instantly.

### Anti-rules
- Do not fill every pixel with noise.
- Do not confuse rebellion with random glitch effects.
- Do not destroy the structural silhouette at `extreme` intensity.

## 4. `focus_attention`

**Narrative thesis:** I decide what deserves attention and what is allowed to become visible.

**Primary Variable:** Focus / Visibility

**Coupled Pair:** Focus × Edge

**Stable channels:** face/eye anchor, silhouette, gaze direction, contrast hierarchy.

**Camera default:** curated or resistant at low Agency; becomes aligned or predictive when the subject takes control.

**Color role:** may encode assigned attention versus chosen attention; use territory/ownership rather than random accents.

**Edge role:** edge clarity is attention clarity. Hardness can propagate from gaze or chosen subject.

**Medium behavior:** layered comic rendering with selective sharpness, contrast blocking, controlled texture revelation, print detail appearing only where attention permits.

### State behavior
- **baseline:** normal clear hierarchy and readable subject.
- **pressure:** image attention begins competing with subject gaze.
- **crisis:** wrong objects become sharp or high contrast; focal logic becomes unreliable.
- **decision:** visual silence; one chosen object or gaze anchor becomes decisive.
- **agency:** clarity propagates along subject intention; hierarchy is character-led.
- **resolution:** plural but stable hierarchy; previously suppressed areas can regain presence.

### Anti-rules
- Do not reduce this archetype to Gaussian blur.
- Do not make the whole scene equally sharp in Agency.
- Do not use unrelated kinetic motion as the primary signal.
