# OBSERVED

## O-B004-01 — A hand gesture can become the only major change inside a held visual system

`B004-001–005` preserve nearly the same camera, two-plane depth, warm/cool role-color, environment treatment and subject scale. The clearest change is George's hand opening at `B004-005` while Gwen remains downcast.

The image becomes more communicative without becoming visually louder.

## O-B004-02 — A warm relational field persists when George is no longer visible

At `B004-006–007`, George has left the frame but a pale warm vertical field remains adjacent to/behind Gwen while Gwen continues to hold a cool cyan/blue identity mass. This is direct visual persistence; its interpretation as off-screen relational presence remains provisional until cross-scene validation.

## O-B004-03 — Facial landmarks and facial surface realism are separate variables

At `B004-008–009`, George's brows, eyes, nose, mouth, jaw and hand remain crisp and specific. Yet the face and torso are built from broad simplified warm planes, and the doorway behind collapses into a painterly orange field.

This is not realistic skin rendering with a watercolor filter. It is **graphic landmark precision over reduced surface realism**.

## O-B004-04 — Gwen remains readable without a visible face

At `B004-010`, the entire face is absent. A large cyan hair mass, the neck/shoulder relationship, deep-blue clothing and the closet rhythm preserve identity and state.

## O-B004-05 — Environmental simplification does not require deleting clutter

The wide bedroom at `B004-011` contains many recognizable objects: shelves, books, posters, drum, clothes, furniture and personal material. Instead of removing them, the image groups much of the clutter into narrow value/chroma ranges, lowers edge authority and reserves stronger separation for George and Gwen.

# INFERRED

## I-B004-01 — Gesture can be a lead emotional carrier under held-channel discipline

A small gesture becomes narratively large when camera, color, depth, texture and subject scale are deliberately held. This strengthens the existing **Parameter Dominance Rotation** model.

## I-B004-02 — Color can function as scene memory / off-screen residue

The persistence of the warm field after George leaves suggests that established color territories may survive cuts and carry relationship memory. This is a candidate mechanism, not yet a whole-world law.

## I-B004-03 — Detail Suppression should be reframed as Hierarchical Compression

A binary `detailed vs simplified` model is insufficient. A frame can remain object-rich while perceptually simple if non-focal objects are compressed through:

- value grouping;
- chroma grouping;
- edge softening;
- reduced local contrast;
- sparse contour information;
- focal detail allocation.

This distinction will be especially important when calibrating lived-in Brooklyn environments.

## I-B004-04 — Character role-color scales with camera distance

The close relational split expands into a wide tableau: George becomes a warm left island, Gwen a cool/bright right island, and the room between them becomes a low-energy middle territory. Role-color therefore persists even when camera distance changes dramatically.

## I-B004-05 — Cartoonization is not global softness

The George close-up shows a more useful model:

```text
precise landmarks
+ broad value/color planes
+ reduced continuous skin gradients
+ selective hard edges
+ simplified surrounding field
```

A face can be graphic and highly specific without either photorealism or complete painterly dissolution.

# GENERATIVE RULES

## G-B004-01 — Gesture-led relational beat

```text
establish relationship geometry
→ hold camera / depth / color territories
→ change one hand/body gesture
→ increase local gesture readability only
→ do not add global FX
```

## G-B004-02 — Color-memory hold

After establishing character-relative color ownership, selected color fields may persist after a character goes off-screen. Treat this as `Color Residue`, not as a fresh palette choice on every shot.

## G-B004-03 — Compress before delete

For lived-in environments:

```text
retain meaningful object count
→ group by value + chroma
→ reduce non-focal edge authority
→ suppress local texture
→ preserve only hierarchy-critical landmarks
```

Do not sterilize an environment merely to make it graphic.

## G-B004-04 — Wide relationship as chromatic islands

```text
Character A territory
→ low-energy middle field
→ Character B territory
```

The middle field can carry distance without becoming empty.

## G-B004-05 — Separate landmark precision from surface realism

For stylized faces, control independently:

- `facial_landmark_precision`
- `facial_plane_abstraction`
- `skin_surface_detail`
- `continuous_gradient_suppression`
- `selective_edge_authority`

Identity preservation should not force realistic skin shading.

### New hard failures

Fail the generation if:

- emotional openness automatically causes more saturation/texture instead of a readable gesture change;
- an environment is made generic/empty when it should remain lived-in;
- cartoonization preserves realistic continuous skin gradients underneath surface effects;
- every shot recomputes a fresh palette and loses established color memory;
- camera widening destroys character-relative color roles.
