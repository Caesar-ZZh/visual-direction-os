## Evidence claims

### EV-MIGUEL-009 — Pursuit-state Persistence Through World-scale Decompression

**Observed:** pursuit and Miles identity remain legible when the chase opens into a vast exterior where Miles may occupy only a tiny fraction of the frame.

**Inferred:** chase continuity does not require stable subject scale or stable environmental density.

**Generative rule:** when opening a chase into world scale, preserve a compact target invariant set while allowing host macro geometry and negative space to take temporary authority.

### EV-MIGUEL-010 — Crowd Pressure Can Compress into a Single Near-field Interceptor

**Observed:** the multi-pursuer logic of B028 can hand off to one enormous mechanically distinct interceptor, followed by a brief character-light host-world hold before Miles re-enters.

**Inferred:** pursuit pressure does not require continuously visible crowd occupancy.

**Generative rule:** permit one high-authority interceptor to replace crowd pressure if target state and host-space continuity remain legible. Interceptor identity remains unresolved here.

### EV-MIGUEL-011 — Host Transport as Temporary Action Constraint

**Observed:** at `15:07:48`, a bright white web visibly connects Miles to a flying vehicle while the vehicle retains host-world material ownership.

**Inferred:** infrastructure/transport can shift from background scaffold to contacted relational action object without material inheritance.

**Generative rule:** `hero connector → host transport object → retained host material`; contact/constraint is a spatial relation, not a texture transfer.

### EV-MIGUEL-012 — World Authority ↔ Pursuer Authority Scale Oscillation

**Observed:** `15:07:38/44` give the city overwhelming spatial authority; `15:07:46` separates a large pursuer from tiny Miles across that host field; `15:07:49` gives Miguel extreme near-field authority while Earth-928 remains readable.

**Inferred:** action authority can be redistributed among world, target and pursuer through scale/occupancy changes while identity anchors remain stable.

**Generative rule:** author chase authority as controlled oscillation among `world-scale field ↔ target anchor ↔ interceptor/pursuer mass`, not permanent hero-centered framing.

**Limit:** the sequence supports temporal authority changes but does not justify reconstructing exact camera path or lens motion.

## Belief updates

1. B028's routing-rail model is not sufficient for every Spider Society chase state; exterior Earth-928 can temporarily give the world more authority than either target or crowd.
2. Crowd coherence and chase continuity are separable; a pursuit may compress to one blocker and reopen into character-light world space.
3. Infrastructure now has at least two evidenced action roles: stable routing scaffold and temporary contacted/constraint object.
4. Identity carve-out at enormous scale may collapse to a very small invariant set rather than requiring face detail.
