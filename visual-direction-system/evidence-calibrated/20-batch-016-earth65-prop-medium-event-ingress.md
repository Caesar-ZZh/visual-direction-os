## Evidence additions

### EV-E65-074 — Prop-medium inheritance

**OBSERVED / INFERRED.** Functional props controlled by a medium-distinct entity can inherit that entity's rendering grammar without spreading it to unrelated host-world surfaces or other characters.

**Production rule:** add `prop_medium_owner` and `owner_medium_inheritance`. Determine medium from explicit ownership/function before falling back to world default.

### EV-E65-075 — Local graphic insert scope

**OBSERVED / INFERRED.** A text/paper/comic gag may remain a localized insert inside an entity-owned medium. It does not require full-frame narrative-domain takeover unless narrative/editorial authority actually owns the whole frame.

**Production rule:** add `graphic_insert_scope = local|object|entity|frame`. Fail automatic global paper/comic conversion from a local gag.

**OBSERVED / INFERRED / VALIDATION.** Very tight Gwen/Vulture contact confirms that the overlap edge can remain a high-contrast ownership seam. Medium difference at the contact boundary is a designed separator rather than a defect to smooth away.

**Production rule:** add `contact_edge_contrast` and explicit edge ownership at overlap. Do not texture-average merely because subjects touch.

### EV-E65-077 — Directional event ingress

**OBSERVED / INFERRED.** A localized event override can enter an entity from a causal side and grow spatially while unhit regions preserve the original entity medium.

**Production rule:** add `event_ingress_origin`, `event_ingress_occupancy`, and `unaffected_entity_preservation`. Reject instantaneous whole-body RGB/glitch treatment when the evidence shows progressive ingress.
