## Evidence claims

### EV-TRANS-001 — DNA-origin-to-destination Binding

**Observed:** `15:16:33` identifies a target DNA origin as `EARTH_0042`; immediately adjacent `15:16:34` confirms `DESTINATION LOCKED EARTH-42`.

**Inferred:** the system binds a detected origin identifier to a destination state rather than presenting destination text as unrelated decoration.

**Generative rule:** expose `diagnostic result → origin label → destination lock` as a readable system-state progression when destination is origin-resolved.

**Limit:** target identity, physical entry/transit/exit/arrival, portal geometry and whether destination lock alone causes transfer remain unresolved.

### EV-TRANS-002 — Diagnostic Carrier Handoff before System Confirmation

**Observed:** host-room scan-like beam → eye-scale diagnostic carrier → full-frame graphic burst → explicit origin/destination text.

**Inferred:** causal system readability can hand off across multiple carriers instead of requiring one persistent HUD.

**Generative rule:** permit `diegetic scan → diagnostic close carrier → graphic transition → explicit confirmation UI`; keep full-frame insert ownership unresolved unless independently proven.

### EV-TRANS-003 — Digital Avatar Medium Locality under Host Contact

**Observed:** the cyan/magenta pixel/circuit-bodied Spider operator/avatar, white/cyan connector strands and dark Earth-928 machinery remain three distinct material owners under dense overlap.

**Inferred:** a strongly non-host digital character medium can remain entity-local during close contact with host infrastructure and connectors.

**Generative rule:** maintain `digital entity medium → contact seam/connector → host hardware` as separate channels with independent edge/value/texture ownership.

**Limit:** operator/avatar identity remains unresolved in B030.

### EV-TRANS-004 — Host-system Recovery after Digital Occupancy

**Observed:** `15:16:39` retains the chamber/aperture and consoles without a dominant character; `15:16:40–41` restore digital-avatar occupancy while host architecture remains materially stable.

**Inferred:** local digital occupancy can disappear/reappear without requiring residue or permanent host-surface conversion.

**Generative rule:** preserve host-system material lock before, during and after transient digital occupancy unless explicit contamination evidence exists.
