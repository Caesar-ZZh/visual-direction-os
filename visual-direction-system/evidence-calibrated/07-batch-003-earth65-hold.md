## INFERRED

### Orientation is a boundary variable
A profile turn can change relational geometry without changing palette, camera or texture. The body can literally reclaim or surrender image territory.

### Static observer / moving subject is a quiet-conflict grammar
Locking the observer mass and environment increases the perceptual weight of small rear-subject movement.

### Readability substitution extends beyond the face
When facial information drops to zero, identity can survive through hair silhouette, body proportion, color ownership and selective edge definition.

## GENERATIVE RULES

- Before changing palette or adding effects, test whether **body orientation + occlusion** can express the relational beat.
- For quiet confrontation, allow a **static observer / moving subject** mode: lock camera, foreground mass and environment; spend motion only on the active subject.
- Rear-view identity does not require restoring a face. Protect at least three alternative channels: silhouette/hair, body proportion, color ownership, costume/shape anchor, selective edge.
- Do not escalate visual intensity automatically across consecutive emotional frames. Stable grammar can make micro-performance more legible.
