## Belief updates

1. **Ownership and attention priority are separable.** An owner can remain materially stable while its attentional rank rises or falls.
2. **Spot proximity is not contamination.** Entity-level anomaly scope is now supported across multiple adjacent close-range frames rather than one threshold image.
3. **Agency is not synonymous with motion intensity.** Everyday communication can interrupt action through performance geometry and editorial attention.
4. **Attack/hold/decay is not universal across scene boundaries.** A hard editorial reset may legitimately drop prior-layer residue directly to zero.
5. **UI ownership can hand off across representational domains.** A screen-space message system may become device-native when the physical phone becomes the explicit subject.
