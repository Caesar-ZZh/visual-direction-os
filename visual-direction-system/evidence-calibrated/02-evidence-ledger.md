## Promotion rule

A claim moves from `PROVISIONAL` to stable methodology only after:

1. support across multiple independent scene clusters or a deliberately narrow validated scope;
2. active counterexample search;
3. explicit scope definition;
4. production implications survive constraint reconstruction;
5. generative tests show the mechanism matters beyond palette/texture imitation.
