**Production rule:** track `interaction_layer_owner` independently from world/domain state and support `interaction_layer_persists_across_domain_handoff`. Do not sever a beam/web simply because the world rendering state changes.

### EV-E65-073 — Silhouette-preserving overexposure

**Production rule:** add `impact_bloom_occupancy`, `identity_silhouette_protection`, `body_axis_protection`, and `residual_costume_anchor_count`. Overexposure fails when pose and identity disappear together with surface detail.
